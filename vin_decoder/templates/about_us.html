About Vinex-USA

We built Vinex-USA because we were tired of guessing what was really under the hood. Whether you're buying a used car, checking a listing, or just curious about your own vehicle's history, we give you the facts — fast, accurate, and free of jargon.

Accurate Decoding

Every VIN is decoded against verified manufacturer data, giving you specs you can actually trust.

Instant Results

No sign-ups, no waiting. Enter a VIN and get a full build sheet in seconds.

Built by Enthusiasts

We're gearheads first — this tool exists because we wanted it ourselves.

Our Mission

Buying, selling, or maintaining a car shouldn't require a mechanic's handshake and a leap of faith. Vinex-USA exists to make vehicle history transparent — pulling together make, model, engine specs, drivetrain details, and known common issues into one clean report, all from a 17-character code.

Who We Are

We're a small team of developers and car enthusiasts based in the USA, combining our love of automobiles with a passion for building tools that actually help people. No corporate fluff — just a straightforward decoder that respects your time.
